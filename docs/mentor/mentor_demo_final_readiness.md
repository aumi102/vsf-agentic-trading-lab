# Mentor Demo Final Readiness

This is the final pre-call demo entry point; no additional feature setup is
required before the mentor rehearsal.

## Run and Upload

Run `python scripts/run_mentor_demo_ui.py`, then open
`http://127.0.0.1:8765`.

Upload only:

1. `docs/mentor/mentor_call_brief_2026_06_22.md`;
2. optionally `docs/reports/progress_report.md`.

No raw data, database, secret, build, or temporary files are required.

## Five-Minute Demo Script

- **0:00–0:30:** overview of the agent/tool product and safety gates;
- **0:30–1:30:** show the pending contract remains blocked;
- **1:30–2:30:** show the registry and noop-only enablement gate;
- **2:30–4:00:** capture decisions into a pending, non-persisted draft;
- **4:00–5:00:** confirm the mentor decisions needed for the next reviewed PR.

## Ask the Mentor

Confirm the first strategy family, universe, execution price, transaction cost,
slippage, rebalance cadence, risk rule, position sizing, and holding period.

## Intentionally Blocked

Real strategy execution, Backtrader, optimizer, full VN100, live fetch, database
mutation, broker/live trading, and performance/profitability claims remain
blocked. This demo is research-only and not investment advice.
